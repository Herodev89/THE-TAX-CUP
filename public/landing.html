Questions

Frequently asked

Is the Consolidated Relief Allowance (CRA) still applied?

No. The Nigeria Tax Act 2025 replaced CRA from 1 January 2026. The Tax Cup applies the new ₦800,000 tax-free band and rent relief of 20% of annual rent, capped at ₦500,000.

Which deductions reduce taxable income?

Employee pension (8% of basic, housing and transport), NHF (2.5% of basic), NHIS contributions and rent relief. Loans and other voluntary deductions come out of net pay without reducing tax.

Are NSITF and ITF deducted from employees?

No. They are employer contributions. The Tax Cup shows them — with the 10% employer pension — as part of the cost to the company, never as a deduction from pay.

Where is our payroll data stored?

On the server your organisation runs The Tax Cup on, not in individual browsers, so every authorised colleague sees the same records and the audit trail can't be altered from a browser.

Is this a substitute for professional tax advice?

No. The Tax Cup applies the published rules consistently, but individual circumstances and exemptions vary. Review final figures with your payroll or tax adviser.
