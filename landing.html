Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about The Tax Cup's calculation models, security, and statutory rules.

Under the statutory model, individuals who pay residential rent are entitled to rent relief computed as 20% of their annual rent expenditure, up to a legal ceiling of ₦500,000 per tax year. The Tax Cup automatically calculates 20% of entered annual rent and strictly caps it at ₦500,000 before computing taxable annual income.
Per the Pension Reform Act, mandatory 8% employee pension contributions are calculated on monthly qualifying emoluments: Basic Salary + Housing Allowance + Transport Allowance (excluding discretionary bonuses and other ad-hoc allowances). The Tax Cup isolates these exact components according to your gross allocation settings.
